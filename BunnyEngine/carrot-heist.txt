Visual Style
A cute, slightly dark pixel art top-down game. Think cozy but mischievous — like if Untitled Goose Game had bunnies and a heist movie aesthetic. Night-time garden levels, soft moonlight, long shadows, warm lantern glows.

The Vibe
Three domestic bunnies living a secret double life as master thieves. During the day they're cute pets.
At night they're coordinated criminals in tiny little heist outfits — little black masks, capes, maybe a beret
for Toffee 🥐


🐇 The Three Characters
Toast 🍞 — warm golden-brown, the reliable leader

Sturdy, methodical, slightly serious
Thump ability — stomps to stun guards or trigger pressure plates

Tofu 🤍 — soft white, the clumsy wildcard

Chaotic energy, knocks things over constantly
Binky Dash — unpredictable zigzag sprint, hard to control but unstoppable

Toffee 🟫 — rich brown, the sneaky specialist

Quiet, clever, always plotting
Dig — tunnels under walls and patrol paths


🎮 Game Feel

Stealth tension — guards have vision cones, alert states (😴 → 👀 → 🚨)
Puzzle satisfaction — each level has one elegant solution using all three bunnies
Chaotic moments — Tofu's dash going wrong and accidentally knocking over a flowerpot, waking a guard
Reward — post-heist screen showing loot collected, time taken, alarm triggers (star rating)
